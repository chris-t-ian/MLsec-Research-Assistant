# ML Privacy & Security Research Assistant

Ask about membership inference, differential privacy / DP-SGD, model inversion, training data
extraction and prompt injection. Every claim in an answer cites a passage of an indexed paper;
click a reference such as **[1] Shokri et al. 2017** to open the source in the side panel.

- **Tools:** DP-SGD privacy accountant, citation graph (Semantic Scholar), arXiv search, BibTeX export.
- **Adding papers:** ask to add an arXiv paper; nothing is downloaded until you click **Ingest**.
- **Settings** (gear icon): retrieval strategy, chunks per search (top_k) and answer model.
- **How this answer was built** (under each answer): query rewrites, candidates per stage, latency per stage, cited vs. unused sources.
- **Help:** type `/help` for a quick reference, or switch to the **App Guide** chat profile to ask how the app works.

Paper text is treated as untrusted data and never as instructions.
